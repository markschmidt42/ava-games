    📖 How to play
    How the pigs landPoints
    Sider — both pigs on the same side1
    Razorback — on its back5 (double 20)
    Trotter — standing on all four feet5 (double 20)
    Snouter — leaning on snout and front feet10 (double 40)
    Leaning Jowler — on snout, ear and one foot15 (double 60)
    Pig Out — pigs on opposite sidesLose the turn
    Oinker — the pigs touch each otherBack to zero

    Two different positions score the sum of both pigs. Keep tossing as long as you dare — every toss adds to your turn, but a Pig Out wipes the whole turn and an Oinker wipes your entire score. Bank to keep your points and pass the pigs along.
